Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

JDMart ("we", "us") is operated by [company legal name], [registered address], Sierra Leone. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the JDMart app, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account: your phone number (used to sign in with an SMS code), name, town, and optionally email, profile photo and a short bio.
  • Listings: photos, descriptions, prices and location you add when selling.
  • Orders: items bought or sold, delivery address, phone number for delivery, notes and order status.
  • Payments: online payments are handled by our payment provider, Monime. We receive the payment status, not your PIN or card number.
  • Messages: chats between buyers and sellers.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can notify you about orders and messages.

How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show listings, process orders and payments, deliver notifications.
  • To keep JDMart safe: prevent fraud, review reported listings, and enforce our Terms.
  • To provide support when you contact us.

What other users can see

Your public profile (name, town, photo, bio, ratings, listings) is visible to everyone. Your phone number is shown only if you turn on "Show my phone number to buyers". Sellers see the delivery name, phone and address for orders placed with them.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (hosting, database, sign-in, notifications) and Monime (payments). They process data on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Account → Delete my account. This removes your profile, listings, saved items and personal data.
